# 🚗 Vehicle Theft Analysis Dashboard (SQL + Power BI)

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **vehicle theft patterns** using SQL and Power BI.
The goal is to uncover insights related to theft trends, high-risk regions, vehicle characteristics, and external factors like population.

The project combines **SQL-based business analysis (36 questions)** with an interactive Power BI dashboard for visualization.

---

## 📁 Dataset

* Source: Kaggle
* Domain: Vehicle theft data
* Data includes vehicle details, theft records, regions, and population

---

## 🧠 Project Workflow

### 🔹 SQL Analysis

Developed 36 business questions and solved them using advanced SQL techniques.

### 🔹 SQL Concepts Used
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window Functions (ROW_NUMBER, RANK, etc.)
- Aggregate Functions (SUM, AVG, COUNT)
- GROUP BY and filtering
- Joins for combining multiple datasets
- Data aggregation and trend analysis

---

### 🔹 Data Modeling (Power BI)

* Imported cleaned data into Power BI
* Structured data for analysis and reporting
* Created relationships between relevant tables

---

### 🔹 DAX Measures & KPIs

* Total Vehicles Stolen
* Vehicles Stolen (Latest Year vs Previous Year)
* Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth
* Average Vehicle Age
* Total Vehicle Brands
* Vehicles per 100K Population

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis & Insights

### 🚗 Vehicle Theft Patterns

* Total vehicles stolen: **4,553**
* Significant **YoY increase of ~73%** in theft cases
* Most stolen vehicle types:

  * Stationwagon
  * Saloon
  * Hatchback

---

### 🎨 Vehicle Characteristics

* Most common stolen colors:

  * Silver (~28%)
  * White (~20%)
  * Black (~13%)

* Average vehicle age: **17 years**

* Oldest model: **1940** | Newest: **2022**

---

### 🏷 Brand  Insights

* Most stolen brands include:

  * Toyota
  * Nissan
  * Mazda
  * Ford

* Majority of stolen vehicles are **standard vehicles (4,348)** compared to luxury

---

### 🌍 Location Analysis

* Highest theft region: **Auckland (1,638 cases)**

* Followed by:

  * Canterbury
  * Bay of Plenty
  * Wellington

* Regions with higher population tend to show higher theft counts

---

### 📊 Population vs Theft

* Total population analyzed: **5.12M**
* Vehicle theft shows a **positive correlation with population size**
* Vehicles per 100K people: **89**

---

## 💡 Recommendations

### 🚗 Theft Prevention

* Focus security measures in high-theft regions like Auckland
* Increase surveillance and monitoring in densely populated areas

### 🎯 Target High-Risk Vehicles

* Improve security for commonly stolen vehicle types and brands
* Encourage use of anti-theft devices for older vehicles

### 🌍 Regional Strategy

* Allocate more law enforcement resources to high-risk regions
* Monitor theft patterns in growing population areas

### 📊 Data Monitoring

* Track year-over-year trends to identify sudden increases
* Use dashboards for continuous monitoring and decision-making
